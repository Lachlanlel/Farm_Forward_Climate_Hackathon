Official NSW starting data

Data provided by Climate Branch, NSW Department of Primary Industries and Regional Development on 4 October 2026. Dataset: NSW Combined Drought Indicator / Enhanced Drought Information System II (EDIS II), monthly parish snapshot, 31 August 2026.

The local snapshot contains 7,377 parishes. It is the latest monthly parish export available in the Seasonal Conditions Information Portal at download time. This is not a live feed. Farm Forward always selects the latest bundled snapshot on or before the session start date.

RI, SWI and PGI are percentile indices, not physical percentages. These are parish-level starting conditions, not measurements on an individual property. Any later Farm Forward projections are separate from official NSW monitoring.

Spatial processing

The official GDA94 parish rings are generalised by at most 0.0001 degrees (approximately 11 metres) to keep the offline snapshot practical to host. The lookup does not assign a parish to points within 0.0002 degrees (approximately 22 metres) of a generalised boundary. These locations show data unavailable instead of an uncertain match. Original downloads and checksums are retained in the project. LGA and LLS fields are unavailable in this export and remain empty.

Data use

Official data can contain uncertainty, missing values, and revisions. It is provided for information and is not an individual farm assessment or a substitute for professional advice. NSW DPIRD does not use parish-level CDI data to target assistance.
